Agility Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Agility is a step-tracking game for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it. The short version: your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We don't collect, see, sell or share any of it.

Information Agility uses

  • Step count from Apple Health. With your permission, Agility reads your step count to work out your level. It only reads steps and never adds to or changes anything in Health.
  • Motion & Fitness. With your permission, Agility reads live step counts from your device's motion sensor while the app is open, so your progress updates as you walk.
  • Your start date. Agility remembers the day you first opened it, so it knows which steps count towards your progress.
  • App settings. Such as the badge colour you've chosen as your background.

Where it's stored

  • On your iPhone and Apple Watch. Your iPhone shares your step progress with your paired Apple Watch, and the Watch app shares your level with its watch-face widget, all on your own devices.
  • Your start date is also saved to your own iCloud account, so your progress can be restored if you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone. It is stored by Apple under your Apple ID and is not accessible to us.

What we don't do

  • Agility has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No information from Agility, including Health data, is sent to us or to any third party.
  • Health and motion data is never used for advertising or sold.

Your choices

  • You can turn off Agility's access to Health at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Agility, and to Motion & Fitness in Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your devices. Your step history remains in Apple Health, which you control.
  • You can manage or delete iCloud data for apps in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Children

Agility doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
